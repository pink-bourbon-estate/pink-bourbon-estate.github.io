Toolkits

Live · Coffee

Coffee Notebook

Dial in a shot, scale espresso and brew recipes (turbo, soup, Slayer-style, lever, AeroPress), tick a flavour wheel, keep a grind log and a list of bags, see charts of what you prefer, and find which beans taste of grapefruit, tea or tomato soup. Early days: some recipes are still starting points.

Live · Cooking

Sourdough and Baking Timesheet

Plan bakes and pasta with baker's percentages: every gram, a clock-time timeline, a Sources sheet for what to weigh out, and dough water temperature.

Live · Cooking

Brewing and Fermentation Timesheet

Mead, wine, beer, kombucha, kefir, kimchi, sauerkraut, pickles, jam, chocolate and more: ingredient sheets, a dated timeline and safety notes. Each recipe says whether it comes from a published source or is a starting point.

Live · Radio

BBC Radio 6 Music Collection

Every track played on BBC Radio 6 Music since 2008: shows, artists, charts, downloads, and a way to find that song you cannot name. Open the site

In development · Music

Sightline

A music score reader and practice companion: a PDF-to-MIDI demo (a demonstration, not a converter), a player with a sampled piano and falling notes, and a score lab for markup and simplifying. Starts with piano; guitar tab and other instruments planned. The desktop app is in the Toolkits repository.

In development · Language learning

Language Lab

A demo that analyses any text in Japanese, Slovak, Czech, Italian, Spanish, French, Greek, Arabic or Norwegian: word counts, sentence lengths, phrases, word endings and a known-words list. Daily word collection is planned.

In development · Games

Chess Lab

Learn opening lines move by move and drill them from memory. A small demo: more lines, spaced repetition and an engine are planned.

In development · Maps

Travel Map

Trips and steps with stats and countries visited, route and photo import, and a street map of Great Britain (from monthly-rebuilt open data, no outside server) that highlights the roads you travelled. Work in progress: map areas to vector and 3D downloads, trees, weather and flight layers are planned.
