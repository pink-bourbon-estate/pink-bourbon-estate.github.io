Live · Coffee

Coffee Notebook

Dial in a shot, scale espresso and brew recipes (turbo, soup, Slayer-style, lever, AeroPress), tick a flavour wheel, keep a grind log and a list of bags, see charts of what you prefer, and find which beans taste of grapefruit, tea or tomato soup. Early days: some recipes are still starting points.

Live · Cooking

Brewing and Fermentation Timesheet

Mead, wine, beer, kombucha, kefir, kimchi, sauerkraut, pickles, jam, chocolate and more: ingredient sheets, a dated timeline and safety notes. Each recipe says whether it comes from a published source or is a starting point.
