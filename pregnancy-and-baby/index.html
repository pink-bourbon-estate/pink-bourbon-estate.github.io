Pregnancy and Baby

Track your pregnancy and your newborn: due date, appointments, food, weight and blood pressure, baby movements, a contraction timer that tells practice tightenings from labour and estimates when your baby may arrive, your hospital bag, vaccines, feeds and growth. Follows NHS guidance. Free, in your browser, no account.

About this tool

A small tool in the same spirit as the rest of Pink Bourbon Estate: free, readable, and yours. Everything stays in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere; you can download your data as Markdown text or as CSV spreadsheet files, and switch between metric, imperial and US units at any time. The code is plain JavaScript with comments, in this folder. This is a work in progress, not all tools are actively maintained, and nothing here is medical advice: it never replaces your midwife, GP or health visitor.

Pink Bourbon Estate is a small place for free, open, readable tools. No cookies, no tracking, no adverts, nothing loaded from other sites.

All tools here are works in progress: expect bugs, and not every one is actively maintained.
