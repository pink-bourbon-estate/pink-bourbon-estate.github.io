Player

PianoCraft · PDF to MIDI demo · Player · Score lab

Pick a piece and press Play. Click a bar to loop it; click a later bar to stretch the loop. Slide the difficulty down to hear and see an easier version.

The score is a simple engraving drawn from the MIDI data: two staves, note shapes, no key signature (sharps and flats are written next to the notes) and no rests, so it is a reading aid rather than a publisher's page. The piano is a set of real recordings stretched to fill in the notes between them, with a little room reverb added.

Credits and licences

Music: every piece here is in the public domain. The MIDI files come from the Mutopia Project's typesetters, named on each page. Piano sound: the Salamander Grand Piano, recorded by Alexander Holm, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence (CC BY 3.0). The recordings are kept in this site's own folder; nothing is loaded from another site. Code: the tool's code is open and commented, in this folder. The desktop app that does the real PDF reading is in the Toolkits repository.

Nothing you do here leaves your browser: markup is saved on your device only, and no files are uploaded anywhere.

Pink Bourbon Estate is a small place for free, open, readable tools. No cookies, no tracking, no adverts, nothing loaded from other sites.

All tools here are works in progress: expect bugs, and not every one is actively maintained.
