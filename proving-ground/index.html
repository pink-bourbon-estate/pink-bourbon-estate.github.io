About this tool

Every recipe is the average of published formulas from baking schools and bakeries, worked out with baker's percentages (flour is 100, everything else is a percentage of it). The Sources tab lists every formula with its citation and link. Your plan stays in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Read how and why it was made. The code is plain JavaScript with comments, in this folder.

Pink Bourbon Estate is a small place for free, open, readable tools. No cookies, no tracking, no adverts, nothing loaded from other sites.

All tools here are works in progress: expect bugs, and not every one is actively maintained.
