Score lab

Sightline · PDF to MIDI demo · Player · Score lab

Mark up a score the way you would in pencil: circle the tricky bit, write a reminder, add fingering. Make it simpler with the slider. Your markup stays on this device.

Practice steps

The order the Russian School of Piano Playing approach suggests. If a step is too hard, turn the Simplify slider down and try that step again with less.
