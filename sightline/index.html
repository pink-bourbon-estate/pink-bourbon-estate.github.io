Sightline

A music score reader and practice companion that runs in your browser. It starts with piano: watch a score being read, practise with a real piano sound and falling notes, mark up and simplify the music. Guitar tab and other instruments are planned: the engine only needs notes, so the same pages can show them. Free, no account, nothing leaves your device.

Work in progress, and an honest note: these pages use public-domain pieces stored as MIDI. They do not read your own PDFs in the browser: reading music symbols reliably from a PDF is done by the desktop app in the Toolkits repository. The "PDF to MIDI" page is a demonstration of the steps, not a converter.

1. PDF to MIDI demo

See a page of music being read: staves, barlines, music-font symbols, pitch and rhythm. Then download the MIDI.

2. Player

A sampled grand piano, a position slider, tempo, loop and repeat bars, hands separately, transpose, a metronome and the 1 to 7 difficulty slider.

3. Score lab

Pen, highlighter, text and fingering on the score, a simplified version, and the Russian-School practice steps to tick off.

Credits and licences

Music: every piece here is in the public domain. The MIDI files come from the Mutopia Project's typesetters, named on each page. Piano sound: the Salamander Grand Piano, recorded by Alexander Holm, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence (CC BY 3.0). The recordings are kept in this site's own folder; nothing is loaded from another site. Code: the tool's code is open and commented, in this folder. The desktop app that does the real PDF reading is in the Toolkits repository.

Nothing you do here leaves your browser: markup is saved on your device only, and no files are uploaded anywhere.
