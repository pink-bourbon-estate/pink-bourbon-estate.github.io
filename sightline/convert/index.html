PDF to MIDI demo

Sightline · PDF to MIDI demo · Player · Score lab

Choose a piece, press "Read this page", and watch the steps. Then download the MIDI file or hear it.

This is a demonstration, not a converter. A browser cannot reliably read the symbols in a PDF. Here the score is a public-domain piece we already have as MIDI, drawn as a page; the boxes and labels show what the desktop app finds in a real PDF. For real PDF conversion, use the desktop app (in the Toolkits repository): it reads typeset PDFs by their music-font symbols, and uses Audiveris for scans.

The first 14 notes. Pitch comes from where the notehead sits on the staff; length from its shape (filled or open, stem, flags).

How the real app does it

Typeset PDFs (from MuseScore, Sibelius, Dorico or Finale) draw each symbol with a music font, so the app can read the symbols directly rather than guess from pixels. That is quick and accurate. Scans are different: they are pictures, so they need optical music recognition (Audiveris), which is slower and needs checking. Colour key on this page: blue dashed = staves, amber = bars, pink = symbols.
