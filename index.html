About

An estate is a place that brings things together. Here that means people, ideas and tools, gathered to make helpful, meaningful things that improve everyday life, without a paywall and without being watched.

Pink Bourbon is a rare coffee variety, and coffee is where this began: a good coffee shop is a sense of place, somewhere people and ideas meet over something made with care. Thanks to Taith Atelier for that inspiration. It is called an estate, not an atelier or a studio, because those suggest a single maker. An estate is shared, and a bit gritty.

I cannot write code myself. These tools are made with the help of AI, with a person in the loop deciding what is built, checking that it does what it says, and insisting that it is explained. The aim is for AI to do the building once, so the result is a plain, commented tool that needs no AI to run or to understand. That reduces reliance, and leaves room for the next project, or for none at all. The essay says more.

  • No paywall. Everything is free.
  • No tracking. No cookies, analytics, adverts or accounts, and nothing loaded from other sites.
  • Readable code. Every program is commented in plain language.
  • Open. Free to use, fork and adapt, with credit. Please do not resell it. Privacy and licence.
  • Ethical sources. Public information, gathered slowly and politely, and credited.

All tools here are works in progress. Expect bugs, and not every one is actively maintained. Questions, corrections and ideas go in an issue on the GitHub page of the project in question; there is no email address.

Pink Bourbon Estate is a small place for free, open, readable tools. No cookies, no tracking, no adverts, nothing loaded from other sites.
