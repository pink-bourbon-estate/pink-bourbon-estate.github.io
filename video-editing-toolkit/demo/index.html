Video Editing Toolkit demo

Video Editing Toolkit · Demo

Choose a video, or make a short sample, then try the three jobs: stills by time, the audio track, and compression.

This is a demonstration, not the converter. It runs in your browser with the browser's own tools, so it can only do small versions of the real jobs: frames as JPEG, audio as WAV, and compression as a short WebM preview. The desktop app (in the Toolkits repository) uses ffmpeg, handles any size and format, and writes H.265 MP4, mp3, m4a, aac and opus. Under each demo is the exact ffmpeg command the app would run for your settings. Your video is never uploaded: it stays in this tab.

The same job in the app

  

  
Only the bitrate and width above affect the demo preview. Codec, quality and speed are the app's settings and only change the command. Lower CRF keeps more detail and makes a bigger file. How much a real video shrinks depends heavily on the footage: screen recordings and slides shrink a lot, busy camera footage much less. The "hvc1" tag lets QuickTime and iPhone play H.265 in an MP4. The "replace original" option writes a temporary file, checks it opens, and swaps it in only if it is smaller.





What the app adds


A window you can drop whole folders onto, a command line for scripts, a progress bar and a Cancel button that cleans up half-written files, a check that a video really has audio before it tries, and a clear message from ffmpeg when something fails. It does not bundle ffmpeg: you install that once. Colour, quality and size choices in this demo are examples, not recommendations.
