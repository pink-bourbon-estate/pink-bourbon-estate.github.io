Brewing and Fermentation Timesheet

Plan brews and ferments, from mead and kombucha to kimchi and jam. Choose a recipe and a batch size, and get every amount to weigh, a dated timeline and the safety notes, with several batches on one sheet. Free, in your browser, no account.

About this tool

Each recipe is marked: "from a published recipe" means its main figures come from a page listed on the Sources tab, scaled to your batch size; "starting point" means typical home practice that has not yet been checked against a source. It works like the Sourdough and Baking Timesheet. Your plan stays in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere. This tool does not cover distilling spirits, which is illegal at home in many places including the UK. The code is plain JavaScript with comments, in this folder, with the recipe data in build_data.py.

This is not medical, legal or food-safety advice. Please read the Safety tab. When in doubt, throw it out.
