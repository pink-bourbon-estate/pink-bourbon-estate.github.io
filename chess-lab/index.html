Chess Lab

Learn a line one move at a time, then play it from memory. Free, in your browser, nothing sent anywhere.

A demo of the idea, work in progress. This board only plays the moves stored in each line: it does not check legality and does not play against you. The four lines were written from general chess knowledge: check them against a book or a trusted site before you rely on them.

The plan

Next: many more openings and endgames in a tree you can browse; spaced repetition so lines you get wrong come back sooner (the same idea as the flashcards in the language tool); import and export of PGN files; puzzles; and, later, play against an engine in the browser (Stockfish is open source under the GPL, which has its own conditions). A chess desktop app already exists in the Toolkits repository: this page is the start of a browser version.

Pink Bourbon Estate is a small place for free, open, readable tools. No cookies, no tracking, no adverts, nothing loaded from other sites.

All tools here are works in progress: expect bugs, and not every one is actively maintained.
