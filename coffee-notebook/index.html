About this tool

A coffee tool in the same spirit as the rest of Pink Bourbon Estate: free, readable, and yours. Recipes say whether they come from a published source or are a starting point. Your grind log and flavour ticks stay in this browser and nothing is sent anywhere. The code is plain JavaScript with comments, in this folder. The About tab lists every source and reading it leans on, and why it exists.

Pink Bourbon Estate is a small place for free, open, readable tools. No cookies, no tracking, no adverts, nothing loaded from other sites.

All tools here are works in progress: expect bugs, and not every one is actively maintained.
