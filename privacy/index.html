Privacy and licence

The short version: this site does not collect anything about you, and everything made here is free to use, share and change, with credit.

Last updated 12 November 2026.

Privacy policy

What is collected

Nothing. This website has no accounts, forms, comments, cookies, analytics, advertising, fingerprinting or tracking pixels. It stores nothing in your browser. It does not load fonts, scripts, images or anything else from other websites.

The host

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when your browser asks for a page (such as your IP address and the page requested) and handles it under its own privacy statement. The owners of this site do not receive that information and cannot see who visits.

Travel Map and other servers

The Travel Map\'s roads are read from files on this site, made once a month from open data (Ordnance Survey\'s OS Open Roads and an OpenStreetMap extract), so opening the map contacts no other server. Two optional buttons can contact another server, and only after you press them and confirm in a box that shows exactly what will be sent: "Draw the route by road" sends your step coordinates to the OSRM routing service run by FOSSGIS (routing.openstreetmap.de), and "Fetch roads from OpenStreetMap" (for places outside Great Britain) sends a map box to an Overpass server. Those servers, like any website, can see your internet address, and their policies apply. Your trips never leave your browser otherwise. Road data: Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right; paths and optional lookups © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

Links to other sites

Some pages link to other sites, for example to search for a song on YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud or Bandcamp, to a BBC programme page, to MusicBrainz, or to GitHub. These are ordinary links. Nothing from those sites is loaded here, and they only learn anything about you if you choose to click. Once you leave, their policies apply, not this one.

Tools you download

The programs are designed to run on your own computer. They do not send your data anywhere. Where a tool needs the internet (for example, to fetch public information), it says so in its code comments and README.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the history is visible in the public repository. Questions and requests go in a GitHub issue; there is no email address.
