About Pink Bourbon Estate

An estate is a place that brings things together. Here that means people, ideas and tools, gathered to make helpful, meaningful things that improve everyday life, without a paywall and without being watched.

The name

Pink Bourbon is a rare coffee variety, and coffee is where this began: a good coffee shop is a sense of place, somewhere people and ideas meet over something made with care. A thank-you goes to Taith Atelier for that inspiration. An estate felt right: a bit gritty, a bit rural, a working place rather than a polished brand. It is also why this is not called an "atelier" or a "studio". Those suggest a single maker. An estate is shared.

What gets made here

Small everyday tools that help people connect, create and discover: a way to find a song, to plan a bake, to learn an opening in chess, to practise the piano. Each one is free to download, free to run on your own computer, and written so that you can open it, read it and change it to suit you.

Honest about how it is made

I cannot write code myself. These tools are made with the help of AI, with a person in the loop deciding what is built, checking that it does what it says, and insisting that it is explained. That is deliberate. The aim is for AI to do the building once, so that the result is a plain, commented tool that needs no AI to run, and no AI to understand. Then it can be used offline, for years, by anyone, and the work does not have to be repeated every time. That cuts reliance rather than adding to it, and leaves room for the next project, or for none at all. The essay goes into this properly.

The promises

  • No paywall. Everything is free.
  • No tracking. No cookies, analytics, adverts or accounts, and nothing loaded from other sites.
  • Readable code. Every program is commented in plain language, so a curious beginner can follow it.
  • Open. Free to use, fork, change and adapt, with credit. Please do not resell it. See privacy and licence.
  • Ethical sources. Public information, gathered slowly and politely, from places that allow it, and credited.

Work in progress

All of the tools here are works in progress. Bugs are to be expected, and not every one is actively maintained. If something is wrong, an issue on the project page is the place to say so.

Get in touch

The place for questions, corrections, takedown requests or ideas is an issue on the GitHub page of the project in question. There is no email address and no form.
