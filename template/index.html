Build a page, panel by panel.

Start from blank or from a template, or bring in a page you already have. Add, move and delete tabs and panels, change what is in them, then download the finished page as a single file. Everything stays in this browser.

Import

Imported pages keep their headings, paragraphs and other content (as editable "Custom HTML" panels). Scripts are removed, so a page's interactive parts will not come across: rebuild those from the panels.

No cookies, no tracking, nothing loaded from other sites. Projects are saved in this browser only; download them to keep them.
