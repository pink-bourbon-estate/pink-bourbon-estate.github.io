Sewing and Knitting Pattern Maker

Pick a garment, a fit and your measurements, and get a print-ready sewing pattern, tiled to print at home. The pattern is generated from your numbers, on your own device. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Work in progress: the patterns pass their own geometry checks but have not been sewn up yet, so make a test garment first. Expect bugs; this tool is not actively maintained. Read how it was made.

Knitting gauge (from your swatch)
Knit a swatch, wash and dry it, then count. Type the numbers here (always per 4 in, not per cm).
3 · Fix common fit complaints
All zero = the block as designed. Move a slider only if your test garment shows the problem. Change one thing at a time, as in the videos.
4 · Show on the pattern

Print at 100% (not “fit to page”), margins none/default, then check the 1 in square on the first page. Trim nothing: neighbouring pages overlap by 1/2 in; line up the red crosses and tape.
